- **app:** Plain-language permission rules can be written, edited and removed in the app (#701). The permission panel on the Agents surface has a "Plain-language rules" section after the written grants: write the rule in a sentence, choose read or search, and optionally narrow it to a path inside the workspace. The row says what is missing before Save would be refused. The Config surface lists each agent's plain-language rules in their own group, after the rules that decide in order. Nothing is allowed by a rule until a judge is configured.
