- **protocol, daemon:** Protocol 2.5 adds recovery over the daemon. `runs.recovery` reads how a run could be recovered, `runs.reviewEffect` records a review of a step that may have acted and never finished, and `runs.recover` starts a checkpoint attempt through retry-safe admission and drives it, once per idempotency key. Run views now show `checkpoint` on a run and `reusedFrom`, `reusedChildren` and `effectReview` on a step.
