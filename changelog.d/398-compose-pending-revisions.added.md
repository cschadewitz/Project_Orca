- **app:** A pending text correction can be composed before it is replaced: **Add after pending** keeps the pending text and adds the draft after it, **Replace pending** (still the default) sends the draft alone, and either way the card shows the whole payload the next agent will receive with a line diff against the pending correction. **Edit a copy of the current correction** starts the draft from it, and earlier versions read in the same window can be copied into the draft. Nothing is sent, answered or granted until the submit button; submission keeps the one pending correction and the existing stale-version checks.
