- **app:** the Runs surface has a **Search** view next to Tree and Board. It searches event text and text artifact contents through the daemon's `search`, across all runs or within the selected run, and each hit opens its step, run or artifact by its canonical link. It says when the daemon cut the scan short, and it is visibly unavailable on a daemon that does not answer `search`. Results belong to one connection: a reconnect, another daemon or another grant clears them, and a late reply from before is dropped. A caller limited to some runs is told to search one run at a time and offered the selected run.
