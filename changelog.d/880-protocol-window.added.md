- **protocol:** Protocol 2.1. `hello` negotiates across a one-release compatibility window: a client may offer a `fallback` version from the previous major, and the daemon answers in the highest protocol both sides share. A daemon accepts the current and previous major once a release opens a window; N-2 is refused with a message naming what the daemon accepts. 2.x opens no window, so 1.10 peers stay refused, but a 2.1 daemon is the first that a future 3.0 app can fall back to.
- **app:** The app connects to a daemon one protocol major behind it, reads and writes, and shows a bar saying the daemon is a version behind and that newer features are off until it is updated.
