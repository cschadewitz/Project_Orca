- **agents:** Writes to `orca.permissions.json` no longer take over a lock by its age (#701). Before, a writer that stopped for 30 seconds could lose its lock, resume, and overwrite a newer edit. If ORCA stops in the middle of a write, `orca.permissions.json.lock` now stays, and later writes are refused until you remove it after checking that nothing is writing. Reading the file is not affected.
