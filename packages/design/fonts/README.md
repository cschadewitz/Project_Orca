# Bundled faces

GENERATED by `tools/font-build/build-fonts.js` — do not edit the `.woff2` files or drop new
ones in by hand. Change the script, run `pnpm run design:fonts`, and commit the result.

They are committed rather than built on install because they change perhaps once a year, and
because an app that cannot render its own text without a successful `pnpm install` from the
public registry is not offline-capable. Nothing fetches a font at runtime: `css/tokens.css`
declares each file with a relative `url()` that resolves over `file://`.

## Why there are symbol faces at all

Plex contains none of the status glyphs — ◐ ◆ ✕ ⊘ ▣ ⏸ ◷ ● are simply not in the font, in
either the Sans or the Mono. Left alone they fall through to whatever the OS happens to have,
which is a different typeface per platform rendered at 11px beside the word it belongs to.
`RULES.md` 4 makes the glyph load-bearing, so it cannot be left to the OS.

The two Noto faces are subset to exactly the glyphs ORCA renders and contain no Latin at all,
not even a space, which is why `type.families` can put them in front of Plex without their
metrics ever touching text. `build-fonts.js` fails the build if either one leaks a text
codepoint, and fails if `semantics.json` grows a glyph no bundled face carries.

Two families are needed because no single OFL face covers all twelve: Noto Sans Symbols 2 has
everything but ⊘ (Mathematical Operators is not in its coverage), and Noto Sans Math has ⊘.

**To see it for yourself:** `pnpm --filter @orca/app dev`, then `#type` in the app window.
It renders every glyph the design system declares — large, then at chip size, in both the
sans and the mono stack, with its codepoint and what declares it — and carries a toggle that
swaps the bundled faces for the OS stack. With the faces off, U+23F8 (`held`) renders as tofu
on Linux and the other symbols visibly change weight and geometry. That is the argument for
this directory, and it is worth looking at rather than taking on trust: a glyph that renders
the *same* with the toggle either way is coming from the OS, which means the subset is
missing it. `build-fonts.js` cannot catch that; only this can.
