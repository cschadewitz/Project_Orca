### D4-02: Revision retry operationIds cannot be inspected or reconciled through `admissions.get/reconcile`, which contradicts the frozen sealed-kind dispatch, and the in-progress envelope still advertises "inspect"
- **Doc:**
  - docs/runtime-operator-parallel-contract.md:629-635 freezes "shared service `inspect(operationId…)` and `reconcile(operationId…)` … Wire inventory: `admissions.get` and `admissions.reconcile`, with fixed registered operation kinds".
  - Lines 863-867: "result inspection dispatches to the registered kind".
  - Lines 951-954 (grant mapping): "`admissions.get/reconcile`: dispatch by sealed operation kind".
  - The evidence map (runtime-operator-integration.md:10-16) does not mention any gap. The refusal appears only inside a dated narrative (runtime-operator-integration.md:550-552: "the current public admission receipt dispatcher refuses revision operation kinds"). Lines 78-80 cover continuation kinds only.
- **Code:**
  - packages/daemon/src/admission-retry.ts:121-165 dispatches only `request.submit` and `run.admit`. Every other kind (`revision.submit`, `revision.replace`, `run.continue`) throws `NOT_SUPPORTED` "This operation's receipt dispatcher is not installed" at :163-164.
  - Revision envelopes still return `{state:"in-progress", recovery:"inspect-or-retry"}` (packages/protocol/src/revision-input.ts:109-116), but no wire path inspects a revision operationId.
  - The MCP `get_admission` tool says "Inspect a retry operation by stable operationId" (packages/integrations/src/mcp/server.ts:85-88). Its result schema accepts only Request/direct receipts (packages/protocol/src/operator-mutations.ts:67,77). A `revise_input` operationId therefore fails.
  - Continuations have dedicated `continuations.get/reconcile`, so the practical gap is revision kinds.
- **Variance type:** contradicts-design
- **Severity:** Medium. It misleads on the recovery API: the contract and the in-progress hint promise inspection, but only same-key replay works.
- **Confidence:** High
- **Suggested resolution:** Decide between two options. One is to add a revision-kind receipt dispatcher, as the contract specifies. The other is to state in the evidence map and in docs/revision-input.md / docs/mcp-operator.md that revision operationIds are recoverable only by same-key replay, and annotate the contract.
