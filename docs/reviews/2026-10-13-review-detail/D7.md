## Findings

### D7-01 — Workflow editor "Discard" confirmation breaks the confirm-in-place contract (previously recorded, still open)
- **Doc:**
  - `docs/design/RULES.md` rule 17 and `60-implementation.md:362-380` say the act with no way back arms its row: **Keep** first and focused, **✕ Confirm** in the danger tone, `Esc` through the layer stack.
  - `60-implementation.md:379-380` names "discard a draft" as one of these acts.
  - `50-accessibility-and-keyboard.md:57` says "An armed row disarms (Keep, `Esc`): focus goes back to the control that armed it".
  - `70-workflow-editing.md:58,104` says "the one act here that confirms and wears the danger tone is **Discard**" and "Discarding asks once".
- **Code:** `packages/app/renderer/src/workflow/editor/workflow-editor.tsx:255-264,331-338`
  - One button toggles its label from "Discard draft" to "Discard every change" and takes `tone="danger"`.
  - It has no `✕`, no **Keep**, and is not on the layer stack, so `Esc` does nothing.
  - It disarms only `onBlur`.
- **Variance type:** contradicts-design
- **Severity:** High
- **Confidence:** High
- **Suggested resolution:** Fix the code. Reuse the armed-row pattern (`ClaimRow`), which is also follow-up 21's case for lifting it into `ui/`.
