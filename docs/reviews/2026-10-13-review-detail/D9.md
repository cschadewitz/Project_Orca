## Findings

### D9-01 — `resume_failed` is recorded for unobserved or unauthorized resume outcomes
- **Doc:** `manual-diagnostics-contract/failure-mapping.md:24` limits `resume_failed` to the case where "An already authorized, user-initiated resume failed". Line 55 adds: "Export `resume_failed` only when the resume operation outcome is actually observed". `crash-recovery/windows-native-implementation.md:42-44` describes the built producer as "authorized resume failure".
- **Code:** `packages/app/src/client/daemon-client.ts:544-550` (`runsResume`) wraps the whole call in `catch { observeOwnedDiagnostic(..., "resume", "resume_failed"); throw }`. As a result `resume_failed` is recorded in three cases the contract excludes:
  - **Transport loss** (fetch rejects at `daemon-client.ts:260-275`). The daemon may have performed the resume, so the outcome is not observed.
  - **Pre-dispatch refusal** (`requireAuthentication && !token` at `:250-255`). No resume was ever attempted.
  - **Authorization refusals** (`AUTH_FORBIDDEN`/redirect at `:280-284`). These record `authorization_denied` and `resume_failed` together.
- **Variance type:** contradicts-design
- **Severity:** Medium. Capture is disabled today, but the producer is wired (`index.ts:638`). Once enabled, support guidance (failure-mapping.md:70) would tell a tester "The resume attempt failed" when the resume may have succeeded or never run.
- **Confidence:** High
- **Suggested resolution:** fix code. Emit only on a daemon-returned, authorized error response for `runs.resume`. Leave transport loss and pre-dispatch or auth refusals `unknown`, as the contract requires.
