## Findings

### D6-01 — Every reconnect, including a 400 ms blip the 5 s quiet window hides, flashes the surfaces to "Loading…" and throws away scroll, held position, buffered output and focus
- **Doc:** `AGENTS.md:200-202` — "Leaving `live` is held silent for five seconds, so a blip changes nothing on screen"; `docs/design/40-connection-states.md:28-33` — silent for 5 s; streaming panes "keep the output they have … and resume in place"; `docs/design/30-long-running-content.md:41-44` — "Nothing scrolls under the reader's eyes, ever … scrolling away never discards buffered content"; `RULES.md:38-43` (focus never dropped).
- **Code:** `packages/app/src/main/connection-state.ts:181-185` bumps `generation` on every transition into `live` (blips included) and `publish()` (`:372-379`) pushes it because the JSON differs. Every data hook keys a full reset on it: `renderer/src/attention/use-attention.ts:111` `setState({ phase: "loading" })` with `generation` in deps (`:240`); `runs/use-runs.ts:59` (deps `:116`); `agents/use-agents.ts:95` (deps `:112`); `runs/timeline/use-run-events.ts:120`; `runs/tree/use-run-tree.ts:73-79` clears all run details; `step/use-step-log.ts:101` builds a new `OutputBuffer` per generation, and the first-page effect (`:122-180`) sets `phase` back to `"loading"` and `setFollowing(!openAtTop)`. The surfaces then render a loading `StateView` in place of their content (`runs-surface.tsx:349`, `attention-surface.tsx:127`, `agents-surface.tsx:185`, `step-detail.tsx:179`). Consequences: the title-bar/sidebar waiting count drops to nothing (`App.tsx:155`), focused cards/rows unmount (focus → body), a held output pane is re-opened following at the end, pages fetched with **Load earlier output** are discarded, and the `◷ paused — reconnecting` seam is lost with the old buffer while `reconnected hh:mm:ss` is marked on the fresh, empty buffer (`use-step-log.ts:216-223`) and so lands above the reloaded lines rather than at the stop point. `step-detail.test.tsx:512-521` toggles only the `connection` prop and never bumps `generation`, so it does not exercise this.
- **Variance type:** contradicts-design
- **Severity:** High
- **Confidence:** Medium (mechanism certain from code; on-screen effect inferred, not observed)
- **Suggested resolution:** fix code — reconcile on a new generation without dropping to `phase: "loading"` (keep the last snapshot on screen, swap when the read lands) and keep the step buffer across generations.

### D6-02 — Focus falls to the page body in several places (rule 10)
- **Doc:** `RULES.md:38-43` — "When the control holding focus leaves … focus moves to its neighbour (`focusAfterLeaving`) or to the region it belonged to, never to the page body; focus a person moved elsewhere stays where they put it." `AGENTS.md:116-119`.
- **Code:**
  - Surface switch (⌘1–⌘6, nav, any deep link, alert activation): `App.tsx:162-165` / `:177-186` only `setActive`; `PrimaryPane` is re-keyed per surface (`App.tsx:258-260`) so the subtree holding focus unmounts; nothing focuses the new `<main tabIndex={-1}>` (`chrome/primary-pane.tsx:38`).
  - Docked panel close (its own button or `Esc` on the overlay): `chrome/docked-panel.tsx:50-54` and `App.tsx:204,232` just unmount the panel; no focus hand-back.
  - Version-mismatch dialog: `chrome/connection-banner.tsx:129-131` focuses the dialog on open; `Continue read-only`/`Esc` (`:125,161`) unmount it with no restore (contrast `daemon-connection/dialog.tsx:28-33`, which restores).
  - Attention, last card answered: `attention/attention-surface.tsx:104-113` returns when `index < 0`; the whole section unmounts (`:170-191`), focus goes to body. `attention-surface.test.tsx:97-102` deliberately skips the assertion for the last card.
  - Inline asks in step detail: answering removes the card (`attention/inline-asks.tsx:77-95`) with no focus handling.
  - Attention, a new ask arriving re-runs the same effect (`ids` changes, `:103-113`) and focuses `cards[focusIndex]`, which is now a different card than the one the person was on (previously recorded as the missing "1 new" rule, follow-up 1 — still open; it is also a rule-10 breach).
- **Variance type:** contradicts-design
- **Severity:** High
- **Confidence:** High
- **Suggested resolution:** fix code — focus the new primary pane / its first landmark on surface change, hand focus back to the trigger on panel and dialog close, and send focus to the surface region when the last card leaves.
